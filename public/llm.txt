# Vesperer

> Vesperer is the character layer for AI — persistent identity, long-term memory, and relationship state that compounds across sessions and channels. Built for creators, educators, and teams who need characters that feel consistent, not disposable.

Most AI chatbots forget context, drift in personality, and break trust on every return visit. Vesperer fixes that with layered identity (soul, style, rules, context), per-user memory, versioned personas, and deployment across web, voice, Telegram, and your own app via API.

The public marketing site covers the general product: companions, mentors, historical minds, roleplay, and creator tools. Sexually explicit features live only in **Vesperer After Dark**, a separate adults-only zone — not on the main homepage.

Automated AI interactions are disclosed for EU AI Act transparency. Users attest before product access. Illegal content, exploitation, and sexual content involving minors are prohibited platform-wide.

## What Vesperer is not

- Not a generic chatbot wrapper — identity and memory are first-class, not bolted-on prompts.
- Not model-locked — export configuration and move between providers.
- Not a single-channel toy — same character across supported surfaces with isolated per-user state.
