# Vesperer

> Vesperer turns your business knowledge, voice, and rules into an AI that answers clients with long-term memory — so relationships compound across sessions and channels. Built for operators, creators, educators, and small teams who need continuity, not another forgettable chatbot.

Most business AIs fail when a real customer shows up: they don’t carry your knowledge, they forget the person, and the relationship never compounds. Vesperer fixes that with layered identity (soul, style, rules, context), per-user memory, and self-serve deploy on web, Telegram, voice, and your own app via API.

The public marketing site covers the general product. Sexually explicit features live only in **Vesperer After Dark**, a separate adults-only zone — not on the main homepage.

Automated AI interactions are disclosed for EU AI Act transparency. Users attest before product access. Illegal content, exploitation, and sexual content involving minors are prohibited platform-wide.

## Channels available now

- Web (admin chat + public character links)
- Telegram (BotFather token in UI)
- Chat API (`POST /api/v1/chat` with persona key `vesp_…`)
- CLI / Personas API for AI agents (`vsk_…` account key → `POST /api/v1/personas`)
- Voice demos

WhatsApp and Discord are not available yet.

## What Vesperer is not

- Not a generic chatbot wrapper — identity and memory are first-class.
- Not model-locked — export configuration and move between providers.
- Not a sales-gated core product — signup, configure, Telegram, and API are self-serve.
